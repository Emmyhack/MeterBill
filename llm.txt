# Light Bill Calculator
Purpose: calculate shared electricity bills locally and export a clean PNG.
Privacy: no authentication, backend calculation, analytics, or reading uploads.
Financial truth: web/src/lib/calculations.ts and allocations.ts.
Rounding: HALF_UP to kobo per row; shared totals use deterministic largest remainder; optional nearest-₦100 is last.
Host: Go net/http embeds web/dist and exposes GET /healthz.
Commands: make test, make build, make run.
